What the file can have

A header row, then one contact per row. Columns are recognised by name; others are ignored.

  • Name (or First name and Last name), required
  • Email, Phone
  • Source (matched to your acquisition sources), Source detail
  • Interests, Notes
  • Lifecycle ({{ crm_policy.lifecycle_labels.lead }}, {{ crm_policy.lifecycle_labels.prospect }} or {{ crm_policy.lifecycle_labels.client }})

Imported contacts belong to you or to the pool you choose. To give a contact to a colleague afterwards, use a handover so they accept it.
