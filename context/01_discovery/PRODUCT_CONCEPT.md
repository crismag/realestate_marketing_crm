# Product concept
Revision 24. Proposed concept framing; user scope remains authoritative.
## Problem
Agents with hundreds of recorded/toured clients cannot remember every outstanding relationship. Shared work can become unattended when nobody knows who is responsible.
## Target users
The five existing internal groups in TARGET_USERS.md. Commission-based personnel need relationship/referral context; commission accounting was not requested.
## Alternatives
Use/configure an established CRM: faster operational validation, but vendor workflows/edition limits apply. Build a focused custom CRM: better workflow control and reuse, but requires maintenance and reliable integrations. Spreadsheet plus reminders: low initial effort, weak concurrent claims/history/automation.
User has selected custom project context generation. This comparison does not reopen that choice.
## Proposed outcome and value
A daily work system based on relationship history, clear accountability, current attention reasons and repeatable follow-up rules.
## Boundary
One company, internal use first. Generic contacts/work/automation core; optional industry extensions.
## Primary workflows
Capture; claim; log interaction; create next action; detect unattended work; accepted handover/release; send/deduplicate notifications; reconcile reports.
## Differentiator hypothesis
Make unattended work and next action explicit across individual and pooled clients. This is a product hypothesis, not a claim competitors lack it.
## Central risk
Agents may not consistently record real interactions, making attention signals unreliable. Test whether lightweight logging plus verified integration evidence produces actionable queues.
## Challenge findings
Deterministic rules suffice initially. Unknown email/tracking data and unclear cadence are material risks. A large visual rule designer or microservice fleet does not test agent adoption. Mobile/offline/real-time needs and operating cost limits are unknown.
## MVP proposal
Demonstrate capture-to-attention with synthetic then authorized pilot data, including a toured client with no task and a pending handover. Measure false/missing attention items with users before scaling features.
## Success metrics
Declared follow-up coverage, unanswered inquiry aging, next-action coverage and correctly reconciled outcomes; baselines/targets not agreed.
## Exclusions
Public portal, tenancy, payouts, inventory and AI scoring remain outside initial scope. Revisit only with explicit need and impact review.
